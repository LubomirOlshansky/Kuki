空気

Keep the air fresh.

Kūki puts your room’s CO₂ in the Mac menu bar, live, and rings a soft wind‑bell when it’s time to open a window.

Free on Mac, iPhone and iPad · Works with IKEA ALPSTUGA via Apple Home

On the Mac

  • Live in the menu bar

    CO₂ sits next to the clock and updates every minute. Show temperature, humidity or PM2.5 there instead if you like. Click it for the whole picture.

  • A chime when it turns stale

    A soft fūrin (風鈴, wind‑bell), plus a notification if you want one, when CO₂ crosses your limit. While the air is stale, a small mark sits beside the reading.

  • Records around the clock

    Even with Kūki’s window closed, your Mac keeps listening while it’s awake, and builds a history for every room you move the sensor to.

Clear air is a short stroke. Stale air closes the circle.

Indoors, CO₂ is the clearest sign that a room needs air. Kūki draws it as an ensō (円相), the one-stroke circle of Zen calligraphy, and tells you plainly what to do.

  • 澄 Clear air

    Like outside. Nothing to do.

  • 和 Fine

    Comfortable. Air it out soon.

  • 淀 Getting stale

    Open a window for a few minutes.

  • 濁 Stuffy

    Ventilate now. Focus and sleep suffer.

Works with the IKEA ALPSTUGA through Apple Home.

Add the sensor to the Home app once and Kūki reads it from there. No IKEA hub, IKEA app or account.

  1. IKEA ALPSTUGAair-quality sensor
  2. Thread
  3. HomePod or Apple TVyour home hub
  4. Apple Home
  5. KūkiMac · iPhone · iPad

A Mac reaches the sensor only through a home hub with Thread. An iPhone 15 Pro or later can also reach it directly when it’s nearby. Which do I need?

And in your pocket.

On iPhone, Kūki adds Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a Live Activity and StandBy. Station Mode turns a spare iPhone or iPad into a full-time monitor. With iCloud sync, your iPhone shows the history your Mac records.

On iPhone and iPad, Kūki records while it’s open. Apple Home only updates the app in front. For a full day of data, leave a spare device in Station Mode, or let your Mac record and turn on iCloud sync.

Paste a week of air into a chat.

Kūki exports a Markdown brief made for Claude or ChatGPT: context, a summary per room, and the data averaged so it fits in a conversation.

Or take every sample at full resolution as CSV, for Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.

And the quiet details.

  • 居間

    Rooms

    Tell Kūki which room the sensor is in. Carry it around and it keeps a history for each one.

  • 記録

    History

    3 hours to 30 days. One-line takeaways, averages, peaks and time over your limit, per room.

  • 一目

    Widgets & Live Activity

    Home Screen, Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and StandBy. They fade when a reading gets old.

  • 雲

    iCloud sync

    Optional. History, rooms and the latest reading move between your devices through your private iCloud.
